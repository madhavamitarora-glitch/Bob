Twice Daily
The Loop

It runs whether
you open it or not

A scheduled job fetches the feed, rescores every stored event against your current interest list, and pushes a single batched notification for anything new that clears the threshold. On a quiet day it sends nothing — silence is the correct output.

  • 01Fetches the full Localist feed twice a day, at roughly 7am and 5pm Eastern.
  • 02Rescores everything stored, so editing your interest list retunes past events immediately.
  • 03Batches up to five new matches into one notification, never five separate buzzes.
  • 04Marks what it sent, so the same event never reaches you twice.
